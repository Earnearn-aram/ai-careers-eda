Thailand vs the world

Thailand evidence is thin. This page shows what exists and states plainly what does not.

Demand

  • Indeed AI Tracker: no Thailand series (covered: AU, CA, DE, FR, GB, IE, IT, NL, US).
  • Lightcast / AI Index share with an AI skill: Thailand is not among the 22 economies covered. Singapore and Hong Kong are the nearest APAC comparators.
  • JobsDB Thailand counts: search counts depend on the query text and date, and queries like "AI" mix in trainers and sales roles. The site returned 403 to the build environment, and earlier counts were not recorded with an exact query and date, so none are entered. data/processed/thailand_jobsdb_snapshots.csv and aicareers-log-snapshot let a like-for-like series accumulate from now on. No trend can be drawn today.

Pay (local currency first; USD at the stated ECB rate)

The only verified Thai pay figure is for the generic Software Engineer role on Levels.fyi, shown as context. Role-specific Thai figures (AI, data) are unverified, from earlier notes, and shown only when confidence C is included. Levels.fyi Thai medians depend on a self-selected sample.
