Thailand: what the evidence shows (limited)

Thai evidence is thin, so this page does not try to compare Thailand with the world quantitatively. It lists what exists, labels recruiter statements as statements, and says what is missing. For global patterns see the overview.

Demand

  • Indeed AI Tracker: no Thailand series (covered: AU, CA, DE, FR, GB, IE, IT, NL, US).
  • Lightcast / AI Index share with an AI skill: Thailand is not among the 22 economies covered. Singapore and Hong Kong are the nearest APAC comparators.
  • JobsDB Thailand counts: search counts depend on the query text and date, and queries like "AI" mix in trainers and sales roles. The site returned 403 to the build environment, and earlier counts were not recorded with an exact query and date, so none are entered. data/processed/thailand_jobsdb_snapshots.csv and aicareers-log-snapshot let a like-for-like series accumulate from now on. No trend can be drawn today.

The Lightcast chart of 22 economies on the overview is the nearest comparison for APAC (Singapore and Hong Kong); it has no Thailand series.

Pay (local currency first; USD at the stated ECB rate)

Beyond the Robert Walters ranges above, two verified Thai pay points exist, both context rather than AI or data roles: the generic Software Engineer median on Levels.fyi (self-reported, n shown) and the top of Michael Page's monthly range for Solution Architect (monthly, from its 2026 guide). Role-specific Thai figures for AI and data roles are unverified and appear only when confidence C is included. For other regions see the overview.

Pay: Robert Walters salary calculator (Bangkok, Tech & Transformation, permanent)

Annual basic salary ranges, excluding benefits and bonuses, from Robert Walters' online calculator for the 2025 and 2026 guides. Ten of the 82 entries for this filter were captured, including the four AI roles. These are the recruiter's benchmark ranges from its own placement data and public information, not a sample of workers. The AI Engineer, Senior AI Engineer, Head of AI and Chief of AI ranges are the same in the 2025 and 2026 guides. Among the other captured roles, three changed: the lower bound rose for Head of Product, and the upper bound rose for Head of Software Development and Senior Product Manager; the rest are unchanged. Two data points do not make a trend. The Senior AI Engineer range starts higher and ends higher than the AI Engineer range, which is the only seniority comparison available for Thailand.

The same page reports headline results of Robert Walters' 2026 survey for Tech & Transformation in Thailand. Sample size, survey period and who responded are not stated on the page. It also rates demand for talent in this sector as High, with AI/ML Engineer, Digital Transformation Consultant and Digital Product Manager as the three most sought-after roles, and gives an average tenure of 2.3 years.

What Thai recruiters say (statements, not measurements)

Robert Walters Thailand (hiring guides of 22 and 30 Dec 2025; the pay data sits in a salary survey that requires a request form) says hiring slowed in 2025, with companies scaling back mid-level hiring to favour senior candidates, and expects AI/ML engineers to be among the roles with the biggest demand in 2026. It names manual QA testers, level-1 support, data-entry operators and junior developers as the roles most exposed to AI because their tasks are repetitive, and expects entry-level salaries to be stable while mid- to senior-level raises are larger. A consultant is quoted on 280,000 new jobs across smart electronics, electric vehicles and semiconductors, with no source given and not specific to AI. Michael Page Thailand says unemployment is under 1% and candidates have strong bargaining power; no AI, data or ML role appears in its 2026 top-10 highest-paying list (the only technology role is Solution Architect). These are recruiters' views and expectations from commercial firms that earn fees from placements, not samples of workers or postings.
